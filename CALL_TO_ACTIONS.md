# 40 Call-to-Action Ideas for Warming Room Client Intake System

## Client Registration & Management
1. **Register New Client** - Quick registration form for new clients entering the warming room
2. **Update Client Profile** - Modify existing client information and preferences
3. **View Client History** - Access complete history of client visits and services used
4. **Search Client Database** - Find clients by name, ID, or other identifying information
5. **Check Client Status** - Verify current client status and eligibility for services

## Check-In/Check-Out Operations
6. **Quick Check-In** - Fast check-in process for regular clients
7. **Bed Assignment** - Assign available bed or sleeping space to client
8. **Check-Out & Feedback** - Process client departure and gather feedback
9. **Visitor Sign-In** - Register visitors and volunteers entering the facility
10. **Emergency Check-In** - Priority check-in for emergency situations

## Services & Resources
11. **Request Meal** - Order meals for clients (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
12. **Book Shower** - Schedule shower time slots for clients
13. **Laundry Service** - Request laundry services for client belongings
14. **Get Clothing** - Access clothing bank and request items
15. **Medical Assistance** - Request medical or nursing support
16. **Mental Health Support** - Connect with mental health professionals
17. **Addiction Services** - Access addiction support and counseling
18. **Housing Support** - Get help finding permanent housing
19. **Job Search Help** - Access employment assistance resources
20. **Legal Aid** - Connect with legal support services

## Emergency & Safety
21. **Emergency Alert** - Trigger emergency alert to staff
22. **Report Incident** - Document incidents or concerns
23. **Request Security** - Call for security assistance
24. **Medical Emergency** - Activate medical emergency protocol
25. **Fire/Evacuation** - Initiate emergency evacuation procedures

## Communication
26. **Send Message to Staff** - Internal messaging system
27. **Schedule Appointment** - Book appointments with case workers or counselors
28. **Request Callback** - Ask staff to return call
29. **Language Interpreter** - Request interpretation services
30. **File Complaint** - Submit formal complaint or concern

## Information & Resources
31. **View Available Services** - Browse all available services and programs
32. **Check Bed Availability** - Real-time bed and space availability
33. **Today's Schedule** - View daily schedule of activities and meals
34. **Community Resources** - Access directory of community services
35. **Transportation Info** - Get transit schedules and transportation help

## Administrative & Reporting
36. **Generate Daily Report** - Create daily occupancy and services report
37. **View Statistics** - Access usage statistics and analytics
38. **Export Data** - Export client data for reporting purposes
39. **Manage Inventory** - Track supplies, food, and resources
40. **Staff Scheduling** - Manage volunteer and staff schedules

## Additional Quick Actions
- **Weather Alert** - Get current weather updates (critical for warming room operations)
- **Contact Emergency Services** - Quick dial for 911, crisis lines
- **Donation Management** - Track and manage incoming donations
- **Volunteer Sign-Up** - Register new volunteers

- **Print Documents** - Generate and print necessary paperwork

- **Payment Processing** - Handle any fees or donations

## Usage Notes

### Priority Levels
- **Critical**: Emergency-related CTAs (Emergency Alert, Medical Emergency, Fire/Evacuation)
- **High**: Daily operations (Check-In, Check-Out, Bed Assignment, Request Meal)
- **Medium**: Support services (Medical Assistance, Housing Support, Job Search)
- **Low**: Administrative tasks (Reports, Statistics, Settings)

### Mobile Optimization
All CTAs are designed to work seamlessly on mobile devices with:
- Touch-friendly button sizes (minimum 44x44px)
- Clear, readable text
- Icon support for quick recognition
- Swipe gestures for common actions
- Offline capability for critical functions

### Accessibility Features
- Screen reader compatible
- High contrast mode support
- Keyboard navigation
- Voice command integration
- Multiple language support

### Implementation Recommendations
1. Group related CTAs together for better user experience
2. Use color coding for different action types (green for positive, red for emergency, blue for information)
3. Implement quick action shortcuts for frequently used CTAs
4. Add search functionality for finding CTAs quickly
5. Allow customization of CTA order based on facility needs
6. Include confirmation steps for critical actions
7. Provide undo functionality where appropriate
8. Track CTA usage to optimize interface over time
